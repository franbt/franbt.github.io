Helios Pavilion

Location Zurich
Footprint 420 m²
Materials Brushed Steel, Glass

Helios Pavilion is a daylight-driven exhibition space conceived as a quiet threshold between interior gallery rooms and the surrounding courtyard.

Its primary structure relies on tensioned steel ribs and translucent panels that diffuse direct sunlight into an even, glare-free wash across the floor plane.
