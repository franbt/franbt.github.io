Client Presentation Overview

Helios Spatial Pavilion was conceived as a multi-device presentation deck for architectural stakeholders. Rather than relying on heavy pre-rendered video clips that break on non-standard screens, the system uses live Three.js procedural geometry and responsive CSS containers. Whether a client opens the link on an iPhone in portrait mode, an iPad at a café, or an 85" conference room TV via wireless casting, the layout adjusts geometry and typography instantly without distortion.

HTMX seamlessly injects this case study view into the DOM without a page reload, automatically triggering the Three.js container mount lifecycle and attaching pointer and touch controls.
