Technical Implementation

The Aether Monolith demonstrates full portrait vertical ratio behavior. Built around a 7-tier parametric cylindrical assembly with sine-based harmonic oscillations, the adaptive FOV calculation in three-stage.js dynamically alters the camera projection matrix to guarantee that the full height of the 3D model stays framed regardless of user screen dimensions.
